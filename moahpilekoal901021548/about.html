ABOUT US

Lesotho Tourism was founded in Lesotho QUTHING by Mr Mohapi Lekoala and Liteboho Phera. The main purpose of this COMPANY is to promote Lesotho's tourism industry by sharing information about the country's beautiful mountains, cultural heritage, tourist attractions, traditional food, and exciting activities. The website aims to help local and international visitors discover the beauty of Lesotho, learn about its traditions, and explore its unique destinations. Through this platform, we hope to attract more tourists, support local businesses, and contribute to the growth of tourism in Lesotho.
